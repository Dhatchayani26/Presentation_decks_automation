LLM-Powered Post-Sales Support Chatbot 
AI-powered post-sales insurance support agent that automates high-volume customer service workflows while enforcing safety, compliance, and intelligent human escalation.
Core Capabilities:
Automates 15–20 post-sales journeys: claim registration, claim status, KYC, policy/document requests, cancellation, and refund status.
Intelligent escalation for sensitive, high-intent, or complex customer interactions.
Ambiguity and fraud-risk detection with proactive human handoff recommendations.
Secure integration through native ACKO APIs with scoped data access.
Compliance-first architecture with no unnecessary data persistence and predefined safety guardrails.
Business Impact:
Up to 60% of inbound post-sales queries automated.
Reduced cost per support ticket through support automation.
Faster customer response times, especially during claim surges.
Improved operational scalability while minimizing brand and compliance risks from manual SOP-driven support.
