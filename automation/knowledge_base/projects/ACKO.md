## Features

Personalized Proactive Assistance – Predict customer needs and proactively notify them about claim updates, renewals, missing documents, or pending actions.
Multilingual Voice Support – Enable customers to interact with the support agent through voice in multiple Indian languages.
Real-Time Claim Guidance – Guide customers step-by-step through the claim process and dynamically adapt based on their claim type and situation.
Predictive Escalation – Identify customer frustration, urgency, or potential dissatisfaction early and route the conversation to the right human support team.
Omnichannel Support – Provide a consistent support experience across WhatsApp, web chat, mobile app, and voice channels.
Document Intelligence – Automatically read and extract relevant information from uploaded policy documents, bills, and claim-related documents.
Customer Sentiment Monitoring – Detect changes in customer sentiment during conversations and trigger proactive intervention when required.
AI-Powered Resolution Recommendations – Suggest the most appropriate next action to support agents based on customer history and ongoing conversations.
Post-Interaction Insights – Automatically summarize conversations and identify recurring customer issues, service gaps, and process improvement opportunities.
Personalized Self-Service Dashboard – Give customers a single place to track claims, policies, pending actions, and support requests.
