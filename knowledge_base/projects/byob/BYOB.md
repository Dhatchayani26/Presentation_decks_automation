
Executive Overview & Definition
What it is: BYOB (Build Your Own Brain) is Akaike’s flagship productized Decision Intelligence Platform and conversational AI co-pilot.
Core Value Proposition: It eliminates decision delays and BI ticket backlogs by allowing business users to ask questions in natural, plain English without needing SQL or query language skills.
Key Functionality: It queries underlying enterprise databases, reasons across disparate systems, and returns accurate, explainable answers paired with auto-generated charts, root-cause analyses, and actionable recommendations.
Core Architecture & How It Works
BYOB moves from raw enterprise data to decisions through a four-to-five-step flow:
Connect (Data Ingestion): Points directly to structured data repositories, enterprise apps, and unstructured files where they live.
Data Sources: Snowflake, Redshift, MSSQL, PostgreSQL, MySQL, BigQuery, Databricks, AWS S3, SAP/ERP, Veeva, SFA, DMS, POS, CRM, claims, Open Payments, PDFs, Excel, and CSV files.
Auto-Joins: Automatically detects keys and table relationships across systems to handle cross-functional queries natively.
Contextualise (Semantic & Knowledge Layer): Codifies organization-specific glossary terms, table relationships, and custom business rules.
Retail/CPG Examples: Teaches BYOB precise definitions for metrics like active SKU, same-store sales, making charges, and NTB (new-to-brand) customers.
Pharma Examples: Resolves brand loyalty, therapeutic area rules, and prescription metrics (TRx, NRx, NBRx, NPS, DOT).
Analyse & Reason (Agent Layer): Uses a smart clarifier and multi-step reasoning agent. It generates a plan before execution, allowing for self-fixing on errors and voting-based quality control.
4 Analytics Levels: Handles Descriptive (what happened), Diagnostic (why it happened/root cause), Predictive (what will happen), and Prescriptive (what move to make next) in a single ask.
Act & Automate (Outputs & Pushing):
Outputs: Generates report-ready charts, one-pagers, slides, and executive dashboards in a single click.
Delivery Channels: Pushes scheduled daily/weekly briefs, real-time anomaly alerts, or direct chat responses to Microsoft Teams, Slack, Email, or embeds into custom enterprise mobile/web apps via APIs.
